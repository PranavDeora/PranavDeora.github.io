Shazlo Island · founded Aug 2025

Swipe to style.

Shazlo is a swipe-based fashion discovery app for Millennials and Gen Z. Instead of scrolling through endless catalogues, you see one piece at a time and swipe on what you like. You can save your finds into closets, share them with friends, and buy them right in the app.

We built Shazlo for small, local, artisan and sustainable labels that get lost on big marketplaces. It gives them a place to be found by shoppers who are likely to love what they make.

I co-founded Shazlo with Darsh Modi, Maurya Samanta and Nabyendu Das, and I lead it as CEO.

₹75,000grant through Ahmedabad University Venture Studio (SSIP 2.0)

Erasmus Island · 41.65°N 4.72°W

Five months that changed me

Before Valladolid I had never left India. I landed in Madrid nervous and unsure whether I could manage alone. Within weeks I was cooking every day, running a house, handling bank payments and budgets myself. Erasmus+ was much more than an exchange semester.

8.13/10average grade at Valladolid
35guests at the Indian dinner I hosted, with garba lessons
4,300walkers at Marcha Asprona, the charity march I helped run
30young people from 9 countries at Click for Change

What I did

  • Elected class representative

    For International Trade, by anonymous vote. Helped Professor Santiago Enciso run the course's company visits and extracurricular activities.

  • Shared India

    Hosted an Indian dinner, gave a talk on India and taught garba. Recorded the ESN Valladolid podcast Nation to Nation: India with Pranav.

  • Click for Change! Young People Against Digital Hate

    A fully funded six-day Erasmus+ youth exchange in Benicàssim on hate speech and misinformation, with travel and stay covered. We stayed at a youth hostel right on the beach. I was India's only participant, and it was my first time at a beach.

  • Learned beyond class

    Food Innovation Hackathon, European Values Session, III Interuniversity Conference on Development Cooperation. At Casa de la India I found out that Ahmedabad and Valladolid are sister cities.

  • Staff volunteer, 49th Marcha Asprona

    On 9 May 2026, 4,300 people joined a 20 km charity run, walk and march in Valladolid to support people with intellectual disabilities. I was one of the staff.
